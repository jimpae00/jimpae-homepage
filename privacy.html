Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-30

Jimpae Peanut Profile helps viewers verify and link their livestream identities so they can view their Peanut Pass status across Jimpae stream platforms.

Information we collect

When you sign in or connect an account, we may store the following account identifiers and public profile metadata:

  • Twitch user ID, username, and display name.
  • YouTube channel ID, channel handle, and channel display name.
  • Discord user ID and display name.
  • Peanut Pass seasons connected to your viewer profile.

We do not collect your Google password, Twitch password, or Discord password.

How we use the information

We use this information only to:

  • Verify that you own the Twitch, YouTube, or Discord account you connect.
  • Show your Peanut Pass seasons on your profile page.
  • Link your viewer identities across Jimpae stream platforms.
  • Help automate Discord role status for Peanut Pass where applicable.

Google / YouTube data

If you sign in with YouTube, we request read-only YouTube access to identify your signed-in YouTube channel. We use it to read your YouTube channel ID and basic channel metadata only.

We do not upload, edit, delete, or manage your YouTube videos, playlists, subscriptions, comments, or analytics.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your linked identity data with advertisers or data brokers.

Unlinking and deletion

You can unlink connected platforms from the Peanut Profile page where available. Unlinking removes that platform identity from your Peanut Profile but does not delete your Peanut Pass history.

To request deletion or correction of linked identity data, contact Jimpae at jimpae00@gmail.com.

Security

Authentication is handled through the official Twitch, Google/YouTube, and Discord OAuth login flows. We store only the account identifiers needed for the Peanut Pass profile system.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: jimpae00@gmail.com
